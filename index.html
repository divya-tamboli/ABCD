ETA estimation

Five synthetic remaining-time observations per route are sampled every 30 seconds. The rolling median is widened by one minute at each end; the traffic scenario adds its delay to the same trace-based estimate. Held-out mean absolute error is 2.1 minutes across 10 simulated trips. The route diagram is schematic and not to scale.

Hard constraints

Passenger allocation cannot exceed seated vehicle capacity (Route B: 32 seats, spare Bus 15: 40 seats). The optimizer checks spare-vehicle conflicts, arrival plus 5-minute turnaround between consecutive trips, departures inside 8:00–10:00, and connection holds against 5 minutes of slack.

Baseline & improvement

Baseline is the modeled weekday timetable and vehicle assignment defined for this simulation. Each scenario compares its baseline with the proposed plan on the same synthetic weekday, using the fixed 8:00–10:00 AM window. Waiting time is weighted by simulated rider counts; on-time percentage is calculated from GPS-trace trip runtimes with a 5-minute tolerance. These are reproducible synthetic results, not live fleet measurements.

Demand forecast

Boardings are forecast by stop and 15-minute window using synthetic weekday counts. Disruption controls modify route runtime, passenger demand, or vehicle availability. The campus-event scenario adds 24 reproducible synthetic riders at Science Quad in the 08:30 window. Stop-only passenger cohorts board the first available service at that stop; destinations and alighting are not modeled, so onboard loads are conservatively carried to the end of a trip. The transfer cohort is added to the demand forecast. No live fleet or student data is used.
